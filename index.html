About Me

I am currently a PhD candidate in Communication at the University at Albany (SUNY). Works collected here serve to demonstrate my professional capabilities in both scientific research and broadcast production.

As a communication scientist, I use computational modeling and EEG to explain how emotion, cognition, and learning interact under various media contexts. As an experienced TV & commercial video producer with 10+ years on the job, I am experienced in planning, multi-camera storytelling, and post-production workflows. This dual expertise informs both my academic and teaching practices.
